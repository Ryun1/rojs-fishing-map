    How the scores work
    • Each species is scored from 0 to 100 on season, sea temperature, wave height at the spot, wind, time of day against sunrise and sunset, tide movement and the kind of spot.
    • Wave height is adjusted for each spot. Swell coming from the direction a coast faces counts in full, swell from behind the island counts for much less, and harbours get a little extra shelter.
    • Species pictures are from your own catches, except the moray, which is a drawing. Species windows and spot facings are starting estimates. Spot positions are approximate. They are meant to be corrected with local knowledge and, later, with catch logs.
    • Moon phase is shown for planning and feeds the best-times timeline, but it does not change the species scores yet. Tide is computed on the phone for Horta from published tidal constants, for any date and with no connection, and matches the official Instituto Hidrográfico table to within a few minutes and about 5 cm. It is the astronomical tide only: wind and air pressure can move the real sea level. You can override it with the dropdown. Madalena is within 7 minutes of Horta, and the other Pico spots are treated as Horta. Tidal constants: TICON-4 (Hart-Davis, Dettmering and Seitz, CC BY 4.0). Live sea data: Open-Meteo Marine.
