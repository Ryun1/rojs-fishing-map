    How the scores work
    • Each species is scored from 0 to 100 on season, sea temperature, wave height at the spot, wind, time of day against sunrise and sunset, tide movement and the kind of spot.
    • Wave height is adjusted for each spot. Swell coming from the direction a coast faces counts in full, swell from behind the island counts for much less, and harbours get a little extra shelter.
    • Species pictures are from your own catches, except the moray, which is a drawing. Species windows and spot facings are starting estimates. Spot positions are approximate. They are meant to be corrected with local knowledge and, later, with catch logs.
    • Moon phase is shown for planning and feeds the best-times timeline, but it does not change the species scores yet. Tide state comes from the official Instituto Hidrográfico table for Horta (2026), and from the Open-Meteo model for other dates. You can override it with the dropdown. Madalena is within 7 minutes of Horta in the table, and the other Pico ports are treated as Horta. Tide tables: Instituto Hidrográfico. Live sea data: Open-Meteo Marine.
